Column 1: The Problem

The "Safe Route" Trap & Permanent Regret

Early in life, most individuals choose traditional or "safe" majors due to financial fear or parental pressure. Decades later, they are locked into golden handcuffs—wondering what life as an investigative journalist, bio-architect, drone wildlife pilot, or surgeon would have felt like.

High Barrier to Exploration

Traditional paths force you to quit your job or spend 2–4 years in university just to discover if you actually enjoy the daily reality.

Glamorized vs. True Work Reality

Media highlights outcomes, not the grueling day-to-day work environment, leaving switchers vulnerable to shock.

Gatekeeping & Niche Isolation

Niche, high-fulfillment fields (like motorsport telemetry or bio-design) lack clear entry points for mid-career adults.

Outcome: Unfulfilled Potential & Unanswered "What-Ifs"
