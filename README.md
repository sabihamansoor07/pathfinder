# PathFinder: Experience Careers Before Choosing Them

## The Problem

Choosing a career is one of the most important decisions a person will make, yet many students are expected to make that decision with little to no real-world exposure.

Research from the OECD shows that nearly **40% of students are uncertain about their future careers**, and career uncertainty is strongly linked to increased anxiety, lower wellbeing, and poorer employment outcomes later in life. At the same time, many professionals eventually regret the careers they chose, often wishing they had explored other paths before investing years into education and training.

The problem is simple:

**People are expected to choose a future they have never experienced.**

Students often select majors and careers based on assumptions, pressure, or limited information rather than genuine understanding. Existing career websites provide information, but very few allow students to actively experience what a career is really like before committing to it.

---

## The Solution

**PathFinder** is a career exploration platform that helps students discover, learn about, and experience careers before making life-changing decisions.

Our platform guides users through a structured journey:

### Explore → Discover → Learn → Connect → Experience

Students begin with an AI-powered Career Discovery Quiz that matches them with careers aligned to their interests, strengths, personality, and goals.

Each career page provides a complete overview of the profession, including:

* Daily responsibilities
* Required education and skills
* Salary and job outlook
* Advantages and challenges
* Industry insights
* "Day in the Life" experiences
* Career-fit assessments

To continue exploring, students complete a practical skills course related to their chosen career. After earning a completion credential, they unlock access to verified professionals, mentors, career counsellors, and internship opportunities in that field.

Recognising that career uncertainty can impact wellbeing, PathFinder also provides access to mental health support and career guidance, helping students navigate both the practical and emotional aspects of career decision-making.

Unlike traditional career websites, PathFinder does not stop at information. It creates a pathway from curiosity to real-world experience.

### Why PathFinder Matters

PathFinder transforms career exploration from a guessing game into an informed, evidence-based journey.

By combining AI career matching, learning pathways, mentorship, counselling, internships, and professional networking into one platform, students can confidently explore careers before investing years of study and thousands of dollars pursuing them.

Our mission is simple:

**Help people experience careers before choosing them, so they can build futures based on confidence, not uncertainty.**
